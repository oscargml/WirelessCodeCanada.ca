About WirelessCodeCanada.ca

Canadians pay some of the highest wireless prices in the world — and most don't realize how many protections they already have. The CRTC Wireless Code, the Internet Code, and the 2025 Telecom Act reforms give consumers real, enforceable rights. The problem is that those rights are buried in dense regulatory documents almost nobody reads.

We built this site to fix that: to translate the rules into plain language and turn them into action — a complaint letter you can send today, a clear path to the CCTS, and honest guides on switching and bill shock.

Our principles

  • Free first. The core tools are free and stay free.
  • Privacy by design. The generator runs in your browser; we store nothing.
  • Independent. We're not affiliated with any carrier, the CRTC, or the CCTS.
  • Honest monetization. We're funded by ads, affiliate links, and optional low-cost toolkits — always disclosed.

How we keep the lights on

This site is free to run for you because of Google AdSense, affiliate commissions (at no cost to you), and optional digital toolkits sold via Gumroad. That's it — no paywalls on the tools, no selling your data.
