Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about your rights and the complaint process. Still stuck? Contact us.

Is it really illegal to charge a switching fee?

Yes. As of October 30, 2025, amendments to the Telecommunications Act prohibit carriers from charging any fee to cancel service or switch providers. If you were charged one after that date, you're entitled to a refund — generate a refund letter.

What's the maximum data overage charge I can be billed?

Domestic data overage is capped at $50 per monthly billing cycle, and international data roaming at $100, unless you expressly agree to keep going past the cap.

Can my carrier refuse to unlock my phone?

No. All phones must be sold unlocked, and any older locked device must be unlocked free of charge on request.

My plan is "unlimited" but I was charged overage. Now what?

That contradicts your Critical Information Summary. Dispute it citing Sections C/D and F of the Wireless Code — our generator does this for you.

How much does a CCTS complaint cost?

Nothing. The CCTS is a free, independent service and can order compensation of up to $5,000. See our step-by-step guide.

Do I have to complain to my carrier before going to the CCTS?

Yes — give your provider a reasonable chance (about 14 days) to resolve it in writing first. The generator creates that initial complaint.

Do you store or see the information I enter?

No. The generator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is stored, transmitted, or visible to us. See our Privacy Policy.

Is this legal advice?

No. We provide general legal information based on published CRTC codes and federal law. For advice on your specific situation, consult a licensed professional. See our disclaimer.

Can I still owe money after switching?

You can't be charged a switching or cancellation fee, but you may still owe the remaining balance on a device financing agreement — that's separate and still valid.
