How to File a CCTS Complaint — Step by Step

The Commission for Complaints for Telecom-television Services (CCTS) is the independent, CRTC-mandated body that resolves complaints about phone, wireless, internet, and TV services — for free. Here's exactly how to use it.

Before you start: The CCTS will ask whether you've already complained to your provider. Always do that first — our letter generator creates that initial complaint for you.

Step 1 — Complain to your provider first

The CCTS expects you to give your carrier a reasonable chance to fix the issue. Send a written complaint (email or letter), state clearly what you want, and keep a dated copy. Give them about 14 days to respond.

Step 2 — Gather your evidence

  • Your contract and Critical Information Summary
  • The bills showing the disputed charges
  • Your written complaint and any reply (or proof they ignored you)
  • Notes of phone calls: dates, names, what was said

Step 3 — File your complaint with the CCTS

It's free and can be done in about 20 minutes:

  • Online: ccts-cprst.ca (fastest)
  • Phone: 1-888-221-1687 (TTY 1-877-782-2384)
  • Mail/Fax: addresses listed on the CCTS website

Describe the issue, what your provider did, and the specific resolution you want (refund amount, unlock, correction, etc.).

Step 4 — Resolution

The CCTS first refers your complaint back to the provider's senior team. If that fails, the CCTS investigates and can facilitate a settlement. Where warranted, it can issue a binding decision requiring compensation of up to $5,000. Most complaints are resolved within weeks.
