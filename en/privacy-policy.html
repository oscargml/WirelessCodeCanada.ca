Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

WirelessCodeCanada.ca ("we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what we do — and, more importantly, don't — collect.

1. The complaint generator collects nothing

Our complaint letter generator runs entirely in your browser. The information you type (name, provider, issue details, amounts) is used only to assemble your letter on your own device. It is never stored, transmitted to us, or seen by anyone else. Closing the tab erases it.

2. We do not require accounts

There is no login, no registration, and no email capture to use any tool on this site.

3. Advertising (Google AdSense)

We display ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and device identifiers to serve and measure ads, and may show personalized ads based on your prior visits. You can manage ad personalization at Google Ads Settings and learn more at Google's advertising policies.

4. Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate, anonymous traffic patterns (e.g. which pages are popular). This does not identify you personally.

5. Affiliate links

Some outbound links are affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Clicking is governed by the destination site's own privacy policy.

6. Digital products (Gumroad)

If you purchase a toolkit, checkout and payment are handled by Gumroad, which collects the information needed to process your order under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or store your payment details.

7. Cookies

Essential site function requires no cookies. Cookies that are set come from third parties (AdSense, analytics). You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

8. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA / PIPEDA)

Because we don't collect personal information through our tools, there's little for us to hold. For data handled by third parties (Google, Gumroad), exercise your access, deletion, and opt-out rights directly with them. Canadian users are covered by PIPEDA; EU users by the GDPR; California users by the CCPA.

9. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Contact

Questions? Email velmorpub@gmail.com. See also our Terms of Use and Disclaimer.
