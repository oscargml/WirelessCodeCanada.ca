What the Wireless Code protects you from

These protections are the law in Canada — not a courtesy from your carrier.

💸

Surprise overage charges

Data overage is capped at $50/month. Charges must stop until you say yes.

✈️

Data roaming shock

International data roaming is capped at $100/month without express consent.

🔓

Locked phones

All phones must be sold unlocked, or unlocked free of charge on request.

📄

Cancellation fees after 2 years

Charging an early-cancellation fee after 24 months is illegal.

🚫

Switching / cancellation fees

Banned under the Telecom Act — CRTC-enforced since June 12, 2026.

📈

Mid-contract price hikes

Carriers can't change key terms or price of a fixed contract without consent.

Recent CRTC rule changes

  • Oct 30, 2025 — Switching & cancellation fees banned (Telecom Act amendments).
  • 2024 — Price transparency rules tightened for advertised plans.
  • 2022 — Data overage cap ($50/mo) enforcement strengthened.
  • 2015 — Wireless Code expanded; applies to all contracts.
  • 2013 — CRTC introduces Canada's first mandatory Wireless Code.
